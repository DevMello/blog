Hi — I'm **DevMello**. I build software, and I write about it here while it's still fresh
enough to be honest about.

This site is two things stitched together:

- **[Writing](/blog)** — notes, essays and build logs. Mostly written mid-problem rather than
  after the victory lap, which makes them more useful and less flattering.
- **[Projects](/projects)** — the things themselves. What they are, what they're for, and what
  I'd do differently.

## Why write it down

Half of what I learn evaporates within a week if I don't. The other half turns out to be wrong,
and writing is the fastest way to find that out. Publishing raises the stakes just enough to make
me check my work.

## Get in touch

Email is the reliable channel — the address is in the footer. I'm slow but I do reply.

If you'd rather subscribe than check back, there's an [RSS feed](/rss.xml) that carries both
posts and project updates.
